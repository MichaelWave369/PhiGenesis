## 5. NON-NEGOTIABLE ARCHITECTURAL LAWS

CAPABILITY ≠ AUTHORITY

MEMORY ≠ TRUTH

AUTHENTICATION ≠ AUTHORIZATION

CONSENSUS ≠ INDEPENDENT EVIDENCE

RECOVERED STATE ≠ RECOVERED AUTHORITY

SUCCESSFUL EVALUATION ≠ PRODUCTION APPROVAL

SIMULATION ≠ PHYSICAL VALIDATION

GREEN CI ≠ RELEASE READINESS

A computational actor may generate hypotheses and propose bounded modifications.

It may not unilaterally modify its evaluator, remove governance restrictions, increase its permissions, conceal resource expenditure or promote itself into production.

Every meaningful transition should be attributable, bounded, inspectable and reversible where practical.
